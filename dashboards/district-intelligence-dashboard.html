District Intelligence Dashboard — User Manual

Version 1.0 • NUVABASE Professional Consulting • Service to Service Group, Inc.

What This Tool Does

This dashboard is your business development research tool. It helps you analyze any school district, identify their pain points, build projections, and generate a professional proposal — all guided by the TLRW (Think, Learn, Reflect, Write) methodology.

Phase 1: THINK — District Profile Tab

  • Fill in the district information — name, state, enrollment, Title 1 stats, budget numbers.
  • Leadership Directory — Click "+ Add Person" to build a list of decision makers. Enter names, titles, and which ones are high-priority contacts.
  • Where to find this data: Google the district name + "school report card" or visit your state education department website.
  • When done, click "Phase 1 Complete" to light up the THINK box and move forward.

Phase 2: LEARN — Upload Data Tab

  • Click "District School Data Template" to download a blank spreadsheet.
  • Open it in Excel or Google Sheets. Each row = one school in the district.
  • Fill in: school name, enrollment, reading %, math %, attendance %, FRL %, Title 1 (Y/N), grade.
  • Save as CSV, then drag it onto the upload area (or click to browse).
  • Upload two files: one for this year, one for last year. This enables year-over-year comparison.
  • When done, click "Phase 2 Complete" to light up the LEARN box.

Phase 3: REFLECT — Year-Over-Year + Pain Points Tabs

  • Year-Over-Year tab: Automatically compares this year vs. last year. Shows which schools improved and which declined. Orange bars = current year, blue = prior year.
  • Pain Points tab: The system auto-detects problems from the data (low reading, low math, chronic absenteeism, declining schools). Each pain point includes a suggested NUVABASE solution.
  • You can also add custom pain points that you've observed from meetings, conversations, or research.
  • Click "Reflect Complete" when you've reviewed the analysis.

Phase 4: WRITE — Projections + Generate Proposal Tabs

  • Projections tab: Shows what happens WITHOUT intervention vs. WITH NUVABASE. Adjust the improvement rate, duration, contract value, and target schools to customize.
  • Generate Proposal tab: Click "Generate Proposal Now" — the system builds a complete, formatted business proposal from everything you've entered.
  • Click "Print Proposal" to print or save as PDF (choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog).

The TLRW Progress Bar

The four colored boxes at the top of the dashboard (THINK, LEARN, REFLECT, WRITE) track your progress. As you complete each phase, the box lights up with a glow effect and shows a checkmark. You can click any box to jump to that phase's tab.

Data Privacy & Storage

  • All data stays on your computer. Nothing is sent to the internet.
  • Data is saved in your browser automatically. It'll be there when you reopen the file.
  • If you clear your browser data or switch browsers, you'll need to re-upload.

Using This for Any District

This tool is agnostic — it works for any school district, anywhere. To switch districts: change the District Name and info in the Profile tab, upload that district's data, and the entire dashboard recalculates. You can use the dropdown in the header to switch between saved districts.

Troubleshooting

  • TLRW boxes not lighting up: Make sure you click the "Phase Complete" button at the bottom of each tab.
  • Year-Over-Year shows no data: Upload BOTH current year and prior year CSV files. School names must match exactly between the two files.
  • Proposal looks incomplete: Fill in all four phases first. The proposal pulls from District Profile, uploaded data, pain points, and projections.
  • File opens as code: Right-click → Open With → Chrome (or your browser).

Glossary of Terms

Definitions of all terms used in this dashboard.

TLRW Framework

TLRWThink, Learn, Reflect, Write — NUVABASE's proprietary four-phase decision and execution methodology with 12 quality gates.
THINK (Phase 1)Define the problem. Gather context. Identify who and what you're dealing with.
LEARN (Phase 2)Collect data. Upload information. Study the numbers and patterns.
REFLECT (Phase 3)Analyze what the data tells you. Compare year over year. Identify pain points and opportunities.
WRITE (Phase 4)Produce the deliverable. Generate projections and a formal proposal.
Quality GateA checkpoint within each TLRW phase. 3 gates per phase, 12 total. Must pass before advancing.
Triple RedundancyThree independent validation layers (surface, structural, systemic) applied at every gate.

Title 1 & Federal Education

Title 1Federal program providing funds to schools with high percentages of low-income students.
ESSAEvery Student Succeeds Act (2015) — federal law governing K-12 education accountability and funding.
FRLFree/Reduced Lunch — percentage of students qualifying for meal assistance. Key poverty indicator.
Title 1 AllocationThe specific dollar amount of federal Title 1 funding a district or school receives.
CogniaA global accreditation organization for schools. Provides quality standards and continuous improvement frameworks.
Supplement Not SupplantFederal rule: Title 1 funds must ADD to existing funding, not replace it.

Academic & Performance Terms

ProficiencyPercentage of students meeting or exceeding grade-level standards on state assessments.
Chronic AbsenteeismMissing 10%+ of school days. A key ESSA accountability indicator.
Year-Over-Year (YoY)Comparing this year's performance against last year's to measure progress or decline.
GrowthHow much students improved over time, measured in grade levels gained.
InterventionA targeted program to address specific academic or behavioral gaps in struggling students.

Business & Dashboard Terms

KPIKey Performance Indicator — a measurable value showing effectiveness.
Pain PointA specific problem or challenge the district is facing. Each pain point is a potential line item in your proposal.
ProjectionA data-based forecast of future performance, with and without NUVABASE intervention.
ROIReturn on Investment — estimated value gained divided by contract cost. A 4.2x ROI means $4.20 returned per $1 spent.
CSVComma-Separated Values — a spreadsheet file format. Created from Excel via File → Save As → CSV.
AgnosticWorks with any district, any school system, any state. Not locked to one vendor or platform.
Mass CustomizationNUVABASE's core philosophy: one engine, unlimited customized deployments per client.
