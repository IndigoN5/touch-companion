User Manual
1. Getting Started ▶

Welcome to Municipal Intelligence by NUVABASE Professional Consulting.

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of your municipality's workforce, operations, grants, and financial performance. It uses the TLRW (Think, Learn, Reflect, Write) methodology to structure analysis and recommendations.

Quick Start:

  • Begin on the Dashboard tab for an overview of key performance indicators.
  • Navigate between tabs to explore specific areas of municipal operations.
  • Connect data sources in the API Connections tab for live data (or use DEMO mode).
  • Use the ROI Calculator to project potential savings for your municipality.
  • Generate professional proposals with the Generate Proposal tab.

System Requirements: Modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox). Optimized for iPad and desktop viewing. No internet connection required for DEMO mode.

2. Understanding Municipal KPIs ▶

The Dashboard tab displays six critical Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for municipal operations:

  • Workforce Efficiency (78%): Measures productive output relative to total labor hours across all departments. Calculated as (productive hours / total available hours) x 100. Benchmark: 80%+ is considered strong for municipalities.
  • Service Response Time (2.3 days): Average time from citizen service request to resolution. Includes 311 calls, online submissions, and walk-in requests. Lower is better.
  • Grant Capture Rate (62%): Percentage of grant applications that receive funding. Calculated as (awards / applications) x 100. National municipal average is approximately 45%.
  • Budget Utilization (91%): Percentage of allocated budget that is effectively deployed. Values between 90-97% indicate healthy spending without waste.
  • Citizen Satisfaction (7.4/10): Aggregate score from citizen surveys, online reviews, and feedback mechanisms. Scores above 7.0 indicate positive public perception.
  • Active Grants ($18.7M): Total dollar value of currently active federal and state grants. Includes both formula-based and competitive awards.
3. Connecting Government Data Sources ▶

The API Connections tab allows you to integrate live data from government sources:

  • Census Bureau API: Provides population, demographics, and economic data. Register for a free key at api.census.gov.
  • Bureau of Labor Statistics: Employment, wages, and labor market data. Register at bls.gov/developers.
  • Open Government Data Portal: Your city's open data (e.g., data.cityname.gov). Enter the portal URL and any required API key.
  • Grants.gov: Federal grant opportunities and tracking. Register at grants.gov for API access.
  • CSV Upload: If APIs are unavailable, upload data manually in CSV or Excel format.

Note: In DEMO mode, all tabs display simulated data for "Metro City." Connect real API keys to switch to LIVE mode with your municipality's actual data.

4. Analyzing Workforce Data ▶

The Workforce Analytics tab provides a department-by-department breakdown of your municipal workforce:

  • Efficiency Rating: Color-coded green (>80%), gold (70-80%), or red (<70%) for quick visual assessment.
  • Overtime %: Percentage of total hours that are overtime. High overtime may indicate understaffing or scheduling issues.
  • Turnover Rate: Annual employee departure rate by department. High turnover increases recruitment and training costs.
  • Budget: Annual departmental allocation. Use alongside efficiency to identify areas where spending may not align with output.

The TLRW Insights panel at the bottom provides automated analysis of key findings (Reflect phase) and actionable recommendations (Write phase).

5. Using the Grant Tracker ▶

The Grants & Funding tab tracks all active and pending grant applications:

  • Status Badges: Active (green) = currently funded; Applied (blue) = submitted, awaiting decision; Pending (gold) = in preparation.
  • Match Required: The percentage of grant amount the municipality must provide as matching funds. 0% = fully federally funded.
  • Pipeline View: Top KPI cards show total captured ($18.7M) vs. in-pipeline ($9.1M) for a clear funding picture.

Use the strategy insights to prioritize applications based on deadline proximity, match requirements, and potential impact.

6. ROI Calculator Guide ▶

The ROI Calculator projects the financial impact of implementing NUVABASE's TLRW methodology:

  • Operational Savings: Calculated as (Annual Budget x Efficiency Improvement %). Based on documented case studies including $7M saved for Chicago Public Schools.
  • Additional Grant Revenue: Projected from improved capture rate applied to average grant value and application volume.
  • ROI Multiple: Total Annual Impact divided by typical consulting engagement investment ($500K). Demonstrates value proposition for decision-makers.

All inputs are editable. Adjust the values to match your municipality's specific parameters. Results update automatically as you change inputs.

7. Generating Proposals ▶

The Generate Proposal tab creates professional, print-ready proposals for prospective municipal clients:

  • Enter the municipality name, population, primary challenge, key departments, and desired timeline.
  • Click "Generate TLRW Proposal" to create the document.
  • The proposal includes: NUVABASE letterhead, executive summary, TLRW methodology overview, deliverables, timeline, investment terms, credentials, and signature block.
  • Click "Print Proposal" to send to printer or save as PDF.

Tip: For best print results, use landscape orientation and set margins to "Minimum" or "None" in your browser's print dialog.

8. Troubleshooting ▶

Common issues and solutions:

  • Data not loading: Ensure API keys are entered correctly in the API Connections tab. If using CSV upload, confirm the file is in .csv format with headers matching the expected fields.
  • Dashboard shows DEMO data: This is expected behavior when no API keys are connected. The dashboard operates in DEMO mode by default with simulated "Metro City" data.
  • Print layout issues: Use Chrome or Safari for best print results. Set orientation to Landscape for tables and charts. The dashboard automatically hides navigation elements when printing.
  • Tabs not switching: Ensure JavaScript is enabled in your browser. Try refreshing the page. If using an older browser, please update to the latest version.
  • ROI numbers not updating: Click outside the input field after changing a value, or press Tab. Ensure values are positive numbers without commas or dollar signs.
  • Proposal not generating: Ensure the Municipality Name field is filled in. All other fields have default values and are optional.
  • File not opening: If downloaded as ZIP, you must RIGHT-CLICK the ZIP file and select "Extract All" before opening. Do not try to open HTML files directly from inside the ZIP.

Municipal Intelligence Glossary
Block Grant

A large sum of federal money granted to a state or local government with only general guidelines for its use. Block grants provide more flexibility than categorical grants but less federal oversight.

CDBG (Community Development Block Grant)

A federal program administered by HUD that provides annual grants to cities and counties for community development activities, including affordable housing, anti-poverty programs, and infrastructure improvements.

Citizen Satisfaction

A composite metric measuring public perception of municipal services, typically gathered through surveys, digital feedback, and 311 interaction ratings. Expressed on a scale of 1-10.

COPS (Community Oriented Policing Services)

A U.S. Department of Justice program that provides grants to hire community policing professionals, develop and test innovative policing strategies, and provide training and technical assistance.

DBE (Disadvantaged Business Enterprise)

A federal program ensuring that small businesses owned by socially and economically disadvantaged individuals have equitable access to government-funded contracting opportunities.

EMS (Emergency Medical Services)

A system providing out-of-hospital acute medical care, transport to definitive care, and other medical transport services. Typically combined with Fire department in municipal structures.

FTE (Full-Time Equivalent)

A unit of measurement equal to the hours worked by one full-time employee. Used to standardize workforce counts when a mix of full-time and part-time staff are employed.

General Fund

The primary operating fund of a municipality, covering day-to-day government operations. Revenue sources include property taxes, sales taxes, fees, and fines.

HUD (Department of Housing and Urban Development)

A federal agency responsible for national policy and programs addressing housing needs, fair housing laws, and community development. Major source of municipal block grants.

Infrastructure

The physical and organizational structures needed for municipal operations, including roads, bridges, water systems, sewer systems, power grids, telecommunications, and public buildings.

Mill Rate

The amount of tax per dollar of assessed property value. One mill equals one-tenth of one cent ($0.001). Used to calculate property tax obligations for municipal revenue.

OSHA (Occupational Safety and Health Administration)

A federal agency ensuring safe and healthful working conditions. Municipalities must comply with OSHA standards for all public employees and contractors.

Prevailing Wage

The minimum hourly wage, benefits, and overtime paid to workers on public works projects, as determined by the Department of Labor. Applies to all federally funded municipal construction.

RFP (Request for Proposal)

A document issued by a municipality soliciting bids from qualified vendors or consultants for a specific project or service. The formal procurement mechanism for government contracts.

SAM.gov (System for Award Management)

The official U.S. government system for entity registration and contract/grant award information. All organizations receiving federal funding must be registered in SAM.gov.

TLRW Framework (Think, Learn, Reflect, Write)

NUVABASE's proprietary methodology for structured problem-solving and decision-making. Think = strategic planning; Learn = data collection and analysis; Reflect = insight synthesis and pattern recognition; Write = actionable implementation and documentation. Applied across workforce, operations, and grant management.

Workforce Efficiency

A metric measuring the ratio of productive output to total labor input across municipal departments. Calculated as (productive hours / total available hours) x 100. Benchmarks: >80% = high (green), 70-80% = moderate (gold), <70% = needs improvement (red).
