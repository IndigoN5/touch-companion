SECTION 2: TITLE 1 SCHOOLS
========================================================================

2.1 Context

Title 1 schools operate under federal funding rules (Every Student
Succeeds Act) that require specific documentation, reporting, and
compliance tracking. These schools serve high-poverty communities
and receive supplemental federal funds. In exchange, they must
demonstrate measurable student outcomes.

  - The 12-gate quality report demonstrates rigor auditors recognize

SECTION 5: CROSS-VERTICAL COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE
========================================================================

5.1 Common Strengths Across All Three Verticals

  1. Budget Alignment
     All three client types budget for "consulting" and "professional
     services," not "software licenses." NUVABASE is positioned as a
     consulting firm that delivers tools as part of engagements.

  2. Trust-Based Sales
     Schools buy from consultants they know. Cities buy from firms
     they have worked with. Construction companies buy from people who
     understand their industry. The relationship is the product.

  3. Quality Documentation
     The TLRW 12-gate validation report is a competitive differentiator.
     When delivered alongside the tool, it demonstrates:
       - Rigor that auditors recognize (schools, cities)
       - Compliance-grade documentation (construction)
       - Professional methodology (all three)

  4. Repeatability
     Once a tool is built for one school/city/contractor, modifying it
     for the next client is fast. Templates accumulate. The fixer
     validates modifications. Delivery accelerates over time.

  5. Recurring Revenue
     All three verticals have ongoing needs:
       - Staff turnover requires retraining
       - Regulations change requiring tool updates
       - New programs/departments need new tools
     Monthly support retainers are natural.
