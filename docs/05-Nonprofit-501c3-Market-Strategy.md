SECTION 1: WHY NONPROFITS ARE THE STRONGEST VERTICAL
========================================================================

Nonprofits have every problem the other client verticals have — tight
budgets, no technology staff, heavy reporting requirements — plus one
critical differentiator:

They are legally required to demonstrate impact to keep their funding.

Every grant received, every donation accepted, every program operated
requires documentation proving it worked. That documentation
requirement is exactly what TLRW was designed to address: structured
process, validated output, documented rationale.

SECTION 2: NONPROFIT OPERATIONAL NEEDS
========================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Grant Reporting Tools
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every foundation that provides funding requires a report.

Required data points:
  - Number of people served
  - Demographic breakdown
  - Program outcomes (measurable)
  - Budget expenditure vs. allocation
  - Narrative describing activities and results

Current state:
  Most nonprofits compile these manually from scattered spreadsheets,
  sign-in sheets, and email chains. Reports take 10-40 hours each.

TLRW solution:
  Web forms that collect data at the point of service. Database stores
  it. Report generator produces output in the format the funder
  requires. TLRW validates the code. Delivery time per report drops
  from days to minutes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2 Donor Management
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tracking requirements:
  - Who gave, how much, when, which campaign
  - Recurring vs. one-time donors
  - Thank-you letter sent (yes/no, date)
  - Tax receipt issued (yes/no, date)
  - Lapsed donor identification
  - Annual giving summary for year-end

Current state:
  Small nonprofits use spreadsheets. Mid-size pay $2,000-$10,000/year
  for Bloomerang, Little Green Light, or DonorPerfect.

TLRW solution:
  Custom donor tracker with the specific fields each nonprofit needs.
  No features they don't need. No per-seat licensing. Delivered at
  consulting engagement cost, not annual SaaS cost.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.3 Program Outcome Tracking
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Example (youth mentoring program):
  - Number of mentors active
  - Number of mentees matched
  - Hours per match per month
  - Attendance at group sessions
  - Academic improvement metrics
  - Behavioral assessment scores
  - Program completion rate

TLRW solution:
  Forms for data entry at each touchpoint. Dashboard showing real-time
  program status. Automated outcome report for board meetings and
  funder submissions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.4 Volunteer Management
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tracking requirements:
  - Hours logged per volunteer
  - Skills and certifications
  - Background check status and expiration
  - Assignment history
  - Recognition milestones (100 hours, 500 hours)

TLRW solution:
  Volunteer portal with self-service hour logging. Admin dashboard
  with compliance alerts for expiring background checks.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.5 Board Reporting Dashboards
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every nonprofit board meets quarterly and expects to see:
  - Financial summary (revenue, expenses, cash on hand)
  - Program metrics (participation, outcomes)
  - Fundraising progress (vs. annual goal)
  - Strategic plan milestone tracking

TLRW solution:
  One-page dashboard pulling from existing data sources. Updated
  automatically. Board members access via shared link.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.6 Event Management

  - Post-event surveys
  - Revenue reporting

TLRW solution:

2.7 Compliance Documentation
------------------------------------------------------------------------

501(c)(3) ongoing requirements:
  - Annual IRS Form 990 preparation support
  - Conflict of interest policy maintenance
  - Board meeting minutes archive
  - Financial transparency reports

TLRW solution:

Annual reports, community impact summaries, infographics showing

TLRW solution:

SECTION 4: COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGES IN THE NONPROFIT MARKET
========================================================================

4.1 Nonprofits Share

Nonprofit organizations communicate constantly through:
  - Coalitions and alliances
  - United Way networks
  - Community foundation grantee groups
  - Sector-specific associations (human services, arts, education)
  - Professional development conferences

One successful engagement generates referrals. Build a tool for one
food bank, every food bank in the coalition hears about it. Word of
mouth is the primary sales channel in the nonprofit sector.

4.2 The TLRW Report Builds Trust

When a nonprofit executive director receives a tool alongside a
report stating "this was validated through 12 quality checkpoints,
here are the issues found, here is the confidence rating," they
interpret this as:

  - This will hold up in an audit
  - This consultant follows a rigorous methodology
  - I can show this report to my board and my funders

Title 1 auditors, foundation program officers, and nonprofit boards
all understand quality assurance processes. The TLRW report speaks
their language.

4.3 Repeatability Drives Margin

  Engagement 1: Build a program tracking tool for a mentoring org
  Engagement 2: Modify the same template for a tutoring org
  Engagement 3: Modify again for an after-school program

Each subsequent engagement requires less work because the template
exists, the fixer validates the modifications, and delivery
accelerates. Margins improve with every client in the same subsector.
